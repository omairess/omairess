BSRT

Behavioral Sleep Resistance Task — an OSLER-style measure of resistance to sleep onset.

Session

Parameters

Defaults reproduce the standard OSLER protocol: a 1 s stimulus every 3 s, sleep onset scored at 7 consecutive missed stimuli (21 s), 40 min maximum.

A response always extinguishes the light immediately, but never shortens the epoch — the next stimulus still starts a fixed interval after this one, so the task lasts the same for everyone. The response window decides whether a press after the light goes out still counts as a response to it.

Instructions to read to the participant

“A red light will flash every few seconds. Each time you see it, tap the screen or press the spacebar. Please try to stay awake, but do not do anything else to keep yourself awake — no moving about, talking, or singing. Just respond to the light.”

Get comfortable. Respond to every flash.
