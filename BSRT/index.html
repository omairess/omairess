BSRT

Behavioral Sleep Resistance Task — an OSLER-style measure of resistance to sleep onset.

Participant

Name, address and birth date are directly identifying. They are stored in this browser and written into every exported file. For research use, prefer a pseudonymous participant ID and keep the identity key separately under your data-protection plan. Date and time of testing are captured automatically.

Protocol

Defaults reproduce the standard OSLER protocol: a 1 s stimulus every 3 s, sleep onset at 7 consecutive misses (21 s), 40 min maximum.

Regular protocol: 500 ms is the conventional lapse threshold.

A response within the hit window is a hit; a slow hit (beyond the lapse threshold) is a lapse. A response later than the hit window is a miss — its raw reaction time is still recorded, but it does not reset the consecutive-miss run. Responding extinguishes the light immediately without shortening the epoch.

Corrections

Applied to the corrected columns only. Raw values are always exported untouched.

Anticipations are removed first, so they cannot inflate the mean and SD that define the outlier bounds. Bounds are computed within each block, so per-minute corrections use that minute's own mean and SD.

Instructions to read to the participant

“A red light will flash every few seconds. Each time you see it, press the space bar or tap the screen. Please try to stay awake, but do not do anything else to keep yourself awake — no moving about, talking, or singing. Just respond to the light.”
