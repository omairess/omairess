Behavioral Sleep Resistance Task and Psychomotor Vigilance Task.

Participant

Name, address and birth date are directly identifying. They are stored in this browser and written into every exported file. For research use, prefer a pseudonymous participant ID and keep the identity key separately under your data-protection plan. Date and time of testing are captured automatically.

Task

Protocol

BSRT defaults reproduce the standard OSLER protocol: a 1 s stimulus every 3 s, sleep onset at 7 consecutive misses (21 s), 40 min maximum. The PVT default is a 10 min run with 2/4/6/8/10 s intervals.

Regular protocol: 500 ms is the conventional lapse threshold.

A response within the hit window is a hit; a slow hit (beyond the lapse threshold) is a lapse. A response later than the hit window is a miss — its raw reaction time is still recorded, but it does not reset the consecutive-miss run. A response under the false-start threshold is a false start.

Corrections

Applied to the corrected columns only. Raw values are always exported untouched.

False starts are removed first, so they cannot inflate the mean and SD that define the outlier bounds. Bounds are computed within each block, so per-minute corrections use that minute's own mean and SD.
