Session

Protocol

Defaults reproduce the standard OSLER protocol: a 1 s stimulus every 3 s, sleep onset at 7 consecutive missed stimuli (21 s), 40 min maximum.

Timing & calibration

These affect measurement accuracy, not the protocol. Read the “Timing” section of the README before changing them.

The hardware offset is the constant lag between this app issuing a frame and the participant’s finger reaching the key — display pipeline plus panel response plus input hardware. Software cannot measure it. Leave it at 0 unless you have measured it with a photodiode and response box, in which case raw and corrected reaction times are both exported.

Instructions to read to the participant

“A red light will flash every few seconds. Each time you see it, press the space bar. Please try to stay awake, but do not do anything else to keep yourself awake — no moving about, talking, or singing. Just respond to the light.”
