BSRT

Behavioral Sleep Resistance Task — OSLER paradigm, frame-locked timing.

Participant

Name, address and birth date are directly identifying. They are stored on this machine and written into every exported file. For research use, prefer a pseudonymous participant ID and keep the identity key separately under your data-protection plan. Date and time of testing are captured automatically.

Task

Protocol

BSRT defaults reproduce the standard OSLER protocol: a 1 s stimulus every 3 s, sleep onset at 7 consecutive misses (21 s), 40 min maximum. The PVT default is a 10 min run with 2/4/6/8/10 s intervals.

Regular protocol: 500 ms is the conventional lapse threshold.

A response within the hit window is a hit; a slow hit (beyond the lapse threshold) is a lapse. A response later than the hit window is a miss — its raw reaction time is still recorded, but it does not reset the consecutive-miss run. Responding extinguishes the light immediately without shortening the epoch.

Corrections

Applied to the corrected columns only. Raw values are always exported untouched.

Anticipations are removed first, so they cannot inflate the mean and SD that define the outlier bounds. Bounds are computed within each block, so per-minute corrections use that minute's own mean and SD.

Timing & calibration

These affect measurement accuracy, not the protocol. Read the “Timing” section of the README before changing them.

The hardware offset is the constant lag between this app issuing a frame and the participant’s finger reaching the key — display pipeline plus panel response plus input hardware. Software cannot measure it. Leave it at 0 unless you have measured it with a photodiode and response box, in which case raw and corrected reaction times are both exported.

Instructions to read to the participant

“A red light will flash every few seconds. Each time you see it, press the space bar. Please try to stay awake, but do not do anything else to keep yourself awake — no moving about, talking, or singing. Just respond to the light.”
